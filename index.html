Medium Incident Response Timeline Analysis

An analyst exported four related events from a compromised workstation, but the export lost their chronological order. Reconstruct the incident sequence first. Then identify the process directly responsible for the outbound connection.

// STEP 1 — RECONSTRUCT THE TIMELINE
